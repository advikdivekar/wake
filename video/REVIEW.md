# REVIEW: The Last Satellite, product demo (63.1 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps)

## Creative calls
- **Reference mapped to our product.** The anmoldoesmotion reel's arc (dark "too many signals" chaos → lines converge and branch → a ring resolves → a bright product world → proof → a red failure in an editor → claim slot-roll → mark) is rebuilt with our own material:
  - The chaos is **the real nine OPS-SAT channels** (live API telemetry, drawn as polylines), with the reel's highlight-block word swap in the console's marigold.
  - The branches are **segment #204's real waveform**, branching to its measured and cited facts from `/demo/204/steps`.
  - The ring is **the console's own probability ring**, counting to TabPFN's 1.000; the camera dives through its hole into the live console.
  - The reel's red line in the code editor became **Task C's real regression** in the Tinker results. It stays red: the product's brand is saying what it got wrong.
  - The slot-roll claim is "Every line is measured, / cited, / or marked unknown."
- **Real product, never a mockup.** Every UI shot is a CDP screencast of the console running against the **live backend** (`make api` + the llama.cpp adapter on :8081), recorded by `web/record_demo.mjs`. The cursor is drawn at the exact CSS position and time where the recorder clicked (`assets/data/clicks_*.json`).
- **Numbers** come only from `evaluations/` or the live API: 2,123 segments, 303,493 samples, 9 channels, P = 1.000 (#204), P = 0.098 (#1579, missed), AUCPR 0.996 vs 0.979, Tinker A 0.722→0.863, B 0.375→0.542 (n.s.), C 0.962→0.827 (worse), D 0.560→1.000, 0 unsupported root causes, $0.83.
- **Not built:** B "The last transmission" (elegiac, one take from the Elegy timeline) and C "Read twice" (split screen: TabPFN and Tinker until both land on one card). See `STORYLINES.md`.
